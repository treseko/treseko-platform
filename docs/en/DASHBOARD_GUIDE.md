# Dashboard guide

<!-- Language: en -->

The Dashboard brings together a quick view of the project's quality and its active build. It does not modify information by itself: it serves to detect where to act first.

## Before starting

Select a solution, a project and, when applicable, a build from the top bar. The indicators are calculated with that context.

## How to use it

1. Open **Dashboard** from the side menu.
2. Review the coverage, execution, results, bugs and evidence indicators.
3. Use **Refresh** when you need to query the information again.
4. Open the indicator or element that needs attention to continue in the corresponding section.

## Custom views

When your edition allows it, use **Configure view** to choose the blocks you want to see and their order. The change affects your work view, it does not delete project data.

## What to review first

- Blocked or failed cases.
- Open bugs without an owner or without evidence.
- Incomplete coverage between requirements, stories and cases.
- Builds with a negative trend compared to the previous execution.

To interpret the results of a run, see the [execution guide](TEST_EXECUTION_GUIDE.md) and the [execution states](EXECUTION_STATES.md).
